Joining the lab

HEATlab is not currently recruiting for any projects.

This applies to Harvey Mudd students, students at other colleges, high school students, and graduate students. If that changes, positions will be posted on Harvey Mudd's Undergraduate Research Opportunities site. The lab doesn't offer remote or virtual positions.

I'm a high school student. Can I do research or an internship here?

Unfortunately, no. HEATlab does not host high school students, and Jim isn't able to reply to individual requests. We'd encourage you to look for summer programs designed for high schoolers at universities near you.

Can I do research with HEATlab remotely?

No. HEATlab doesn't offer remote or virtual research positions; all lab research happens in person at Harvey Mudd.

I'm a graduate student or applying to PhD programs. Can I join?

Harvey Mudd is an undergraduate-only college, so there are no graduate students, PhD positions, or postdocs in the lab.

I'm a Harvey Mudd student. How can I get involved later?

Any future openings will be posted on the Undergraduate Research Opportunities site (HMC login required). In the meantime, the best preparation is coursework: CS 151 (Artificial Intelligence) and strong programming and math foundations. Harvey Mudd's CS summer research program lists projects across the whole department.

I'm a student at another college. Are there summer positions?

HEATlab has no positions right now. Harvey Mudd's CS summer research program describes opportunities across the department, including any open to students from other colleges.
